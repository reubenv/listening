Install Mission Log to open it from your home screen, even with no signal.

Use it like an app

In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app and works with no signal.

The app keeps its own saved progress, separate from Safari. To bring yours along, open any week here in Safari, switch to Full sheet and tap Save a backup under Your weeks. Then do the same in the app, using Restore from file.
