How this works
  1. Before: do the snapshot at the bottom first. Then read each phrase out loud once, using the sound hint. If you can, read Jeremias 52:6-20 and the Juízes accounts in Portuguese in JW Library.

  3. After: look at your number. Nothing here is graded, and anything you didn't catch simply comes back another week.
